Our Menu

Chicken

Free-range chicken, marinated overnight and cooked to order. Every dish is made with fresh herbs and spices, never frozen meat.

Lemon-Herb Grilled Chicken

A juicy chicken breast marinated in lemon, garlic and rosemary, flame-grilled and served with roasted vegetables and herbed rice.

Crispy Fried Chicken

Golden, crunchy pieces coated in our secret blend of eleven spices, served with coleslaw, warm cornbread and honey-chili dipping sauce.

Butter Chicken

Tender chicken simmered in a creamy tomato and butter sauce with mild spices, served with fluffy basmati rice or fresh naan bread.

Chicken Biryani

Fragrant long-grain rice layered with spiced chicken, caramelized onions, saffron and mint, served with cool cucumber yogurt.

Beef

Premium cuts from trusted farms, aged for tenderness and cooked exactly the way you like it, from rare to well done.

Grilled Ribeye Steak

A thick, marbled ribeye seared over open flame and finished with garlic butter. Served with creamy mashed potatoes and grilled asparagus.

Classic Beef Burger

A thick patty of freshly ground beef with melted cheddar, crisp lettuce, tomato and pickles on a toasted brioche bun, served with golden fries.

Slow-Cooked Beef Stew

Beef shoulder braised for four hours with carrots, potatoes and red wine until it melts in your mouth. A hearty, comforting favorite.

Beef Stir-Fry

Thin strips of beef tossed in a hot wok with peppers, broccoli and ginger-soy sauce, served over steamed jasmine rice.

Sushi

Made fresh throughout the day by our sushi chefs using seasoned rice, crisp nori and the freshest fish and vegetables.

Salmon Nigiri

Two slices of buttery Atlantic salmon pressed over hand-shaped sushi rice with a touch of wasabi. Simple, clean and classic.

California Roll

Crab, avocado and cucumber rolled inside-out in sesame-coated rice. A mild, creamy roll that is perfect for first-time sushi lovers.

Spicy Tuna Roll

Fresh tuna mixed with chili mayo and green onion, rolled with cucumber and topped with crunchy tempura flakes.

Garden Veggie Roll

Avocado, cucumber, carrot and pickled radish wrapped in nori and rice. A light, fresh vegetarian option served with soy sauce and pickled ginger.
